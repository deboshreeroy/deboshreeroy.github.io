Mini ML Refresher Series

A series of short, editorial explainers that distill core ML concepts into product-rooted intuition, with tradeoffs, failure modes, and “how to measure it” baked in.

Overview

This is a communication project, but treated like product work: clarity, examples, and correct evaluation.

Problem

ML explanations online often skip the parts that matter in practice: measurement, tradeoffs, and where things break. I wanted a refresher series that’s genuinely usable — especially for product teams making real decisions.

  • Ground each concept in a real-world example
  • Explain not just “what it is,” but “when it fails”
  • Connect metrics to product outcomes

Format

  • Each entry: concept → metaphor → tradeoffs → failure modes → mini project
  • Short, skimmable sections with consistent structure
  • Visual-first: small diagrams and a single clean example plot

Topics included

  • KNN and decision boundaries
  • K-means clustering and “what even is a cluster?”
  • Ranking + recommenders and evaluation (NDCG, precision@K)
  • Bias/variance and why “more data” isn’t always the fix
  • Model monitoring and what drift looks like in practice

What makes it product-rooted

  • Every concept ties to a decision (ship? iterate? measure?)
  • Metrics are framed as tradeoffs, not scores
  • Failure modes are treated as expected, not exceptional

Artifacts

  • README-style posts (GitHub Pages / Markdown)
  • Lightweight visuals (SVG / screenshots)
  • Mini projects with reproducible notebooks

Next steps

  • Add an index page for the series with tags + navigation
  • Publish as a monthly cadence and track readership
  • Expand into “how to evaluate” templates for common problems
